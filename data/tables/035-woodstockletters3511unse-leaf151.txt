He was not surprised when they came to give him the last Sacraments, but received them calmly and with devotion, with the convi(?tion that he was surely to die, for he assured Father Minister that he would not recover. He shook hands with each of those present in his old cheery way and took leave of them happily. About a half an hour before breathing his last, he gathered all his strength, and cried out, '*My Jesus, I give you my whole heart and soul." These were his last words — a fitting close to a noble life spent entirely for the glory of God. — R. I. P.
