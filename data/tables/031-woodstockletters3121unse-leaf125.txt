"You told them what we weren't able to tell," was what greeted him often on coming down from the pulpit. His obedience was worthy of a Jesuit. He never desired one place more than another, and he went cheerfully wherever he was sent, and though of a very noble and high family he conversed with the lowly as easily as with the others.
